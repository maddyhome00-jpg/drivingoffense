Who we are

A motoring notice practice built around careful attention to detail.

Driving Offence Solutions was established to help motorists understand the notices and allegations that can affect their licence, livelihood and day-to-day lives.

A letter from the police or a court can be difficult to interpret. The language can be technical, the deadlines can be short and the consequences can be significant. People need to understand what the document actually says before they decide how to respond.

The work begins with the detail: what was sent, when it was sent, what information was provided and what the relevant documents show.

The aim is not to promise a particular outcome. It is to approach each enquiry carefully, explain the issues and identify what should be considered next.

How we approach the work

Less assumption. More attention to the facts.

Every motoring allegation has its own circumstances. A useful review starts by understanding those circumstances rather than jumping to conclusions.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the documents

Identify what has been issued, who issued it, what it alleges and what response or action it requests.

02 / EXAMINE

Look at the details

Consider dates, correspondence, procedural questions and the supporting information available.

03 / EXPLAIN

Make the position clearer

Explain the issues in straightforward language so that the person involved can understand what they are dealing with.

04 / IDENTIFY

Understand the next step

Identify what needs attention, what information may be missing and whether specialist legal advice is needed.

05 / BE REALISTIC

No guaranteed outcomes

No responsible review should promise that a notice will be cancelled or that a particular court result will follow.

06 / BE CLEAR

Know the boundaries

Where formal legal advice or representation is required, the appropriate authorised professional should be involved.
